# Articles

Openings of the articles on [beacon-kvm.com](https://beacon-kvm.com). Each one links to the full text.

### [How to access a computer's BIOS remotely](access-bios-remotely.md)

Remote desktop tools stop at the login screen. To reach the BIOS or UEFI setup of a machine you are not sitting at, you need hardware that captures its video and emulates a keyboard.

### [What one onsite visit actually costs](cost-of-an-onsite-visit.md)

A truck roll to fix a machine costs far more than the drive. Work out what one site visit costs you — for most people a single avoided trip pays for the hardware outright.

### [An IP KVM for a homelab](homelab.md)

Homelab machines have no IPMI, live in a cupboard, and break at the worst moment. One bad reboot costs you an evening — and that is the evening this pays for itself.

### [Beacon KVM compared with PiKVM, JetKVM and TinyPilot](ip-kvm-comparison.md)

An honest comparison of four IP KVMs — price, remote access, video, ports and power control. Beacon costs more than JetKVM and does less than PiKVM in one place; here is the whole table.

### [IP KVM vs remote desktop: when you need hardware](ip-kvm-vs-remote-desktop.md)

RDP, VNC, TeamViewer and AnyDesk run inside the operating system. An IP KVM runs underneath it. A plain comparison of what each one can reach, and which you actually need.

### [A Raspberry Pi KVM, or a Beacon device](raspberry-pi-or-a-beacon-device.md)

The same software runs on a Raspberry Pi 4B you assemble yourself and on the Beacon Device we build. Where the two differ: setup, picture quality, bandwidth and support.

### [A remote machine won't boot. Now what?](server-wont-boot-remote.md)

When a server or PC at another site stops booting, remote access dies with it. What you can still do without driving there, and what actually requires hands on site.
